Alan Turing

The father of modern computer science and artificial intelligence

Key Milestones in Alan Turing's Life

  • 1912: Born in London, England.
  • 1936: Introduced the concept of the Turing Machine, laying the theoretical foundation for modern digital computers.
  • 1939–1945: Played a pivotal role at Bletchley Park cracking intercepted coded messages, including the German Enigma machine.
  • 1950: Proposed the famous Turing Test to evaluate machine intelligence in his paper "Computing Machinery and Intelligence".
  • 1954: Passed away, leaving an unmatched legacy in mathematics, computer science, and cryptanalysis.
